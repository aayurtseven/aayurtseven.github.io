Language

The app follows the device language automatically. If your device language is English, the whole UI appears in English. Otherwise switch manually: Settings → Language → English.

Steps — phone / tablet

  1. Open the app. On the first screen tap SIGN IN (Turkish: "Giriş Yap").
  2. Enter the demo email and password above, tap SIGN IN.
  3. The demo playlist downloads automatically ("Loading your list…"). Wait a few seconds.
  4. The channel list appears (ZZ Demo 1–4). Tap any channel — video plays immediately.

Steps — Android TV

  1. Open the app on the TV. Use the D-pad; the on-screen keyboard appears for text fields (a USB keyboard also works).
  2. Select SIGN IN, type the demo email and password, confirm.
  3. The demo playlist loads automatically; press OK on any channel to play.
  4. D-pad: LEFT/RIGHT navigate panels, OK selects, BACK returns. No dead-ends; the app is fully navigable without a touchscreen.

Alternative — no account needed

  1. On the first screen choose Upload Channel List (Turkish: "Kanal Listesi Yükle").
  2. Paste this URL and confirm: https://www.zzxtreamplayer.com/demo/list.m3u
  3. The same 4 public test channels load; tap any to play.
